Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-01-21

This Privacy Policy describes how information is collected, used, and shared when you use the mobile game Idle Fruit Farmer, developed by Toni ("the Developer").

1. Information Collection and Use

The game does not directly collect personally identifiable information such as name, email address, or phone number.

However, the game uses third-party services that may collect certain information automatically for analytics, advertising, and in-app purchases.

The types of data that may be collected include:

  • Device identifiers
  • IP address (processed in anonymized form)
  • Gameplay and usage data (such as session duration and events)
  • Advertising data
  • Purchase-related information (non-sensitive)

2. Third-Party Services

The game uses the following third-party services:

  • Google AdMob (advertising)
  • Google Analytics for Firebase (analytics)
  • Google Play Billing (in-app purchases)

These services may collect and process data in accordance with their own privacy policies.

Google Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

The Developer does not control how these third-party services use the collected information.

3. Advertising

The game displays advertisements provided by third-party ad networks. These ads may use device identifiers to show personalized or non-personalized ads, depending on the user’s device settings and applicable regulations.

Users can manage ad personalization through their Android device settings.

4. In-App Purchases

The game offers optional in-app purchases. All payments are processed securely by Google Play. The Developer does not collect or store payment information such as credit card details.

5. Data Storage

Some game data (such as progress or settings) may be stored locally on the user’s device. This data is not shared with the Developer or third parties, except as described through analytics and advertising services.

6. Children’s Privacy

The game is not specifically directed at children under the age of 13. The Developer does not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children under 13.

If you believe that a child has provided personal information, please contact the Developer so the data can be removed.

7. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated revision date.
